# Brand Voice: Cura Mobility Services

*v1 draft, 2026-09-22. Built on `context.md`. Items marked ⏳ wait on client answers.*

## 01 · Voice overview

Cura talks like the dependable neighbor you'd trust to drive your mother to dialysis. It is calm, kind and specific. It speaks mostly to worried family members and to riders who may be in pain, tired or anxious, so every sentence should lower stress: say what happens, when, and who to call. Cura is professional without being clinical and friendly without being casual about safety. It never hypes, never pressures, and never claims more than it can prove.

**Voice essence:** *Cura sounds like a caring, steady local driver who shows up on time: warm, clear and trustworthy.*

## 02 · Tone dimensions

| Dimension | Where Cura sits | In practice | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual ↔ Formal | Middle, leaning warm | Contractions, plain words, no slang | "We'll call when we're on the way." |
| Calm ↔ Energetic | Calm | No exclamation-heavy excitement; reassurance over enthusiasm | "Your ride is set for 7:30 Tuesday morning." |
| Serious ↔ Witty | Serious with a smile | Friendly warmth, no jokes about health or accessibility | "Running late at the clinic? No problem. Just call us." |
| Accessible ↔ Authoritative | Accessible | Explain Medicaid/Medicare plainly; never lecture | "Medicaid rides are booked through your health plan. Here's how." |
| Distant ↔ Intimate | Close, respectful | "You" and "your loved one"; dignity first | "We'll help your dad from the door to his seat." |

## 03 · Voice qualities

**Reassuring**
Riders and families are often stressed. Lead with what they need to know and what happens next.
- **Do:** "Book by phone or online. We confirm every ride the day before."
- **Don't:** "Transportation solutions for all your mobility needs."

**Respectful**
Riders are people, not "patients" or "cases". Protect dignity in every word.
- **Do:** "Riders who use a wheelchair" / "your loved one"
- **Don't:** "wheelchair-bound", "the elderly", "handicapped person"

**Plain-spoken**
Short words and concrete details beat industry terms. Explain NEMT the first time you use it.
- **Do:** "Non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) means rides to appointments that aren't emergencies."
- **Don't:** "ambulatory NEMT modalities", "leveraging our fleet"

**Honest**
Cura is new, so it earns trust by being accurate, not by inflating. It never invents numbers.
- **Do:** "A new Baton Rouge company, built on doing the basics right."
- **Don't:** "Louisiana's #1 trusted provider" or "thousands of happy riders"

**Dependable**
Reliability is the promise. State it as specific behaviors, not adjectives.
- **Do:** "We'll text you when your driver is 15 minutes away." ⏳ (only if the process is real)
- **Don't:** "We are the most reliable company in town."

**Warm (with a light touch of faith ⏳)**
Care shows in tone. Whether faith language appears depends on ask #9. Default: logo only, no scripture in copy.
- **Do:** "Treated like family, every ride."
- **Don't:** Preachy or exclusionary language.

## 04 · Vocabulary

**Words we use:** ride, rider, driver, on time, door-to-door, appointment, pickup, drop-off, loved one, family, safe, dignity, peace of mind, Baton Rouge (plus the confirmed neighborhoods and parishes), book, call us, confirm, help, wheelchair-accessible ⏳, dialysis ⏳, round trip.

**Words we avoid:** solutions, leverage, world-class, premier, luxury, cutting-edge, best-in-class, revolutionary, #1, unmatched, patients (unless clinical context), the elderly, wheelchair-bound, handicapped, cheap, guarantee (unless contractual), statewide ⏳, licensed ⏳, Medicaid provider ⏳, "years of experience" (no tenure).

**Brand-specific language**
- Customers are **riders**. The people who book for them are **families** or **caregivers**. Facilities are **partners**.
- The company is **Cura** or **Cura Mobility**. The full name is Cura Mobility Services. Drop "LLC" except in legal text.
- A booking is a **ride**, not a "trip request" or "transport".

## 05 · Writing style rules

- **Sentence length:** short and medium. One idea per sentence. Keep most under 20 words.
- **Punctuation:** no em dashes (house rule; use periods, commas or colons). Exclamation marks only in rare, genuine warmth (for example "Thank you!"), never in headlines. No ellipses.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case for headings and buttons ("Book a ride", not "Book A Ride").
- **Numbers:** numerals for times, prices, phone numbers and counts (7:30 a.m., 3 rides a week). Spell out one to nine in prose when they aren't data.
- **Phone format:** (225) 363-0845 ⏳ confirm the public business line.
- **Contractions:** yes (we'll, you're, don't).
- **Voice:** active. "Our driver helps you to the door", not "Assistance is provided".
- **Humor:** only light and warm. Never about health, disability, age or cost.
- **Reading level:** aim for grade 6–8. Many readers are seniors or reading on a phone in a waiting room.

## 07 · Before and after

1. **Before:** "Cura Mobility Services provides premier NEMT solutions across the great state of Louisiana!"
   **After:** "Rides to medical appointments for families in the Baton Rouge area."
   **Why it works:** it's concrete, local and truthful, with no unproven "statewide" or "premier".
2. **Before:** "We transport wheelchair-bound elderly patients."
   **After:** "We help riders who use wheelchairs get to appointments safely."
   **Why it works:** it puts the person first and protects dignity.
3. **Before:** "Trusted by thousands. Licensed and Medicaid-approved."
   **After:** "Questions about Medicaid or cost? We'll walk you through your options."
   **Why it works:** it makes no unverifiable claims and still answers the real worry.
4. **Before:** "Leverage our reliable fleet for all your transportation needs."
   **After:** "We show up on time, help you to the door, and wait if your appointment runs long." ⏳
   **Why it works:** it describes reliability as behavior, not an adjective.
5. **Before:** "Contact us today for a free quote!!!"
   **After:** "Call (225) 363-0845 or request a ride online. We'll confirm the price before you book."
   **Why it works:** it's calm, clear and removes cost anxiety.

## 08 · Things we never say

- Hype: world-class, premier, luxury, #1, best in Louisiana, revolutionary.
- Unverified claims: licensed, Medicaid provider, statewide, years in business, ride counts, star ratings we don't have, "insured" (until documents are received).
- Fear or pressure: "Don't risk missing your appointment!", fake scarcity or countdowns.
- Diminishing language about age or disability: wheelchair-bound, the elderly, suffering from, confined to.
- Competitor put-downs. Contrast with behaviors ("we call ahead"), never names.
- Medical advice. Cura is transportation, not care. Point riders to their doctor or health plan.
