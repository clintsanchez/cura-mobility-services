# Messaging Framework: Cura Mobility Services

*v1 draft, 2026-09-22/23. Built on `context.md` and `voice.md`. For Michael to review and choose. ⏳ = a proof point that is only usable once Michael confirms it's true in operation. Guardrails: no "licensed", "Medicaid provider", "statewide", tenure or invented numbers.*

## 01 · Core message
**Cura Mobility Services helps Baton Rouge families get the people they love to medical care safely and on time, by treating every ride with the patience and respect of family.**

## 02 · Value proposition
Cura drives Baton Rouge-area riders to doctor visits, dialysis, treatments and hospital discharges, and helps them from the front door to the check-in desk. Families get one local number, a driver who shows up on time, and plain answers about cost before the ride. We're a small local company, so every ride gets our full attention.

## 05 · Audience-specific messaging

**Family caregivers (adult children booking for a parent)**
- *What they care about:* Mom is safe, on time and not alone. Easy booking from work. Cost clarity.
- *Lead with:* reliability plus door-to-door help. "We'll take good care of your mom."
- *Proof that lands:* confirmations and the on-the-way call, door-to-door help, a local owner.
- *Avoid:* clinical language, pressure, anything implying they're failing as caregivers.

**Riders (seniors, dialysis patients, post-surgery)**
- *What they care about:* dignity, comfort, not being rushed, a friendly face.
- *Lead with:* respect and patience. "No rushing. We help you every step."
- *Proof that lands:* the same friendly driver where possible ⏳, help with walkers and wheelchairs, waiting when appointments run long ⏳.
- *Avoid:* "elderly", "patients", "wheelchair-bound", tiny type.

**Facility schedulers (dialysis centers, discharge planners, case managers)**
- *What they care about:* dependable pickups, easy scheduling, standing orders, clear communication.
- *Lead with:* "A local ride partner you can count on for recurring and discharge rides."
- *Proof that lands:* recurring schedules ⏳, direct owner contact, insurance and driver documentation ⏳.
- *Avoid:* consumer-emotional tone. Keep it brief and operational.

## 07 · Things not to say
- Unverified credentials: licensed, Medicaid provider/approved, insured (until documents arrive), statewide, "X years", ride counts, star ratings.
- Emergency implications: "ambulance", "24/7 emergency", anything suggesting Cura replaces 911. Add a footer note: "For medical emergencies, call 911."
- Category clichés: "transportation solutions", "your mobility partner", "world-class care".
- Competitor comparisons by name, or implied digs ("unlike other companies that don't show up").
- Medical advice. Cura drives; doctors and health plans advise.
- The consultant's brand language (Mitresonz). Keep Cura's voice its own.
