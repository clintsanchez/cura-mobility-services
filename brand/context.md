# Brand Context: Cura Mobility Services

*2026-09-22. Built from the intake survey, public records, SEO research and Michael's iMessages. ⏳ = pending client answers (CLIENT-ASKS.md). Accuracy guardrails apply: no "licensed", no "Medicaid provider", no "statewide", no tenure, no public address until confirmed.*

## Brand
- **Name**: Cura Mobility Services (legal: Cura Mobility Services, LLC)
- **Category**: Non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT), van. NPI taxonomy "Non-emergency Medical Transport (VAN)".
- **Description**: Warm, reliable non-emergency medical rides by van for families in the Baton Rouge area.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch. LLC formed 2026-08-20, NPI issued 2026-08-26, no website, Google Business Profile or reviews yet.
- **Website**: none yet. Probably curamobility.org (⏳ ownership). The build is a turnkey WordPress site.

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Adult children and family caregivers booking rides for an aging or mobility-limited parent. Also the rider directly: seniors, dialysis patients, people after surgery or discharge, and wheelchair users (⏳ confirm wheelchair vans). Secondary: facility schedulers (dialysis centers, discharge planners, nursing homes, case managers).
- **Key Problem**: Getting someone who can't drive to medical appointments safely and on time, without the no-shows, long waits and rushed handling local reviews complain about. Families also can't tell who pays: Medicaid through brokers, Medicare (generally no), or private pay.
- **Their Language** (from search data and competitor reviews): "non emergency medical transportation", "medical transportation near me", "wheelchair transportation", "dialysis transportation", "rides for seniors", "how much does it cost", "does Medicare/Medicaid pay", "on time", "waited for hours", "didn't show up", "peace of mind", "treated my mom with dignity".

## Positioning
- **Differentiation** (working, ⏳ client to confirm):
  - **Reliability and respect**: on-time pickup, a call or text on the way, door-to-door help, dignity. This directly targets the no-show and long-wait complaints about local competitors.
  - **A local owner who answers the phone**: a Baton Rouge 225 number, not a national template or out-of-state call center.
  - **Clarity**: plain answers on who pays, what it costs and how to book. No local competitor publishes prices.
  - **Care rooted in faith**: the logo has an open Bible with a cross. ⏳ Ask #9: shown on the site, or logo only?
- **Service model**: mirrors his consultant's company, Mitresonz, minus luxury. That means NEMT, ADA/wheelchair and bariatric passengers, everyday rides, and prescription drop-offs and courier. ⏳ Confirm.
- **Competitors**: Mitresonz Transportation (5.0 stars, 185 reviews; the consultant's firm, review leader), Hammond Medical Transportation (4.5, 33; top map-pack spot), Tender Kare Transportation (3.3, 74; Denham Springs), plus national templates (Uber Health, RideSenior, Freedmans Health) and Acadian Ambulance NEMT on the facility side.
- **Market Position**: accessible and dependable, not luxury or budget. A small, personal, local operator, like "a neighbor with a van" but professional.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: dependable, caring, respectful, friendly, steady
- **Tone**: professional but warm. Plain-spoken and reassuring, never clinical or salesy. Survey: Professional, Empathetic/Warm, Friendly/Approachable.
- **Voice Admires**: the client's inspiration sites (Mitresonz, MoRide, CareMove, Golden Rides). He likes a family-focused, "dignity and peace of mind" warmth, a booking CTA up front, and clear statements about who pays.

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values** (working draft, ⏳): Dignity · Reliability · Compassion · Honesty · Community
- **Mission** (working draft): Get Baton Rouge families where they need to be for their health, safely, on time and treated like family.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: 90 days: leads and traffic. 6 months: revenue and growth. Launch a site that ranks locally and converts calls and ride requests.
- **Key Metrics**: phone calls and ride-request form submissions, Google Business Profile reviews (target 3–5 in month 1, then 2–4 a month), organic rankings for core Baton Rouge NEMT terms.
