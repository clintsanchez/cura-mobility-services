## 3. Licensing and enrollment

### What Cura needs (from official sources opened)

1. **Broker credentialing, not a single state "NEMT license."** LDH's Medical Transportation Provider Resources page (https://ldh.la.gov/medicaid/medical-transportation-provider-resources) says: *"The transportation brokers currently oversee the credentialing and/or management of the NEMT provider network."* The current brokers are:
   - **MediTrans** for all five Healthy Louisiana MCOs: Aetna Better Health, AmeriHealth Caritas, Healthy Blue, Humana Healthy Horizons and Louisiana Healthcare Connections. Enrollment line: 1-844-349-4326.
   - **Verida** for fee-for-service (legacy) Medicaid, at 225-726-2800.
   - A provider may contract with one or all of the brokers. *"LDH does not require that each transportation broker accept every potential NEMT provider that applies."*
   - Member-facing confirmation: https://ldh.la.gov/page/medical-transportation
   - **Correction to the brief:** LDH does **not** currently list MTM as a Louisiana Medicaid broker. UnitedHealthcare's LA Medicaid contract ended on 2025-12-31 (secondary: https://mediroutes.com/nemt-state-guide/louisiana).
2. **LA Medicaid Provider Enrollment Portal.** The same LDH page says: *"All NEMT providers are required to register for the Medicaid Enrollment Portal."*
3. **LPSC General License Certificate (Exempt, Non-Emergency Medical).** The LPSC info sheet (https://lpsc.louisiana.gov/docs/trans/Information-for-General-License-Certificates-GLC-for-Non-Emergency-Medical-Carriers.pdf) says: *"The non-emergency medical exempt carriers are the only carriers that are now required to have a GLC from our office."*
   - The process is to obtain a Medicaid provider number first, then submit a notarized medical-exempt affidavit (MT-10).
   - The GLC is what allows the carrier to buy **"for hire" / "public" / "public handicapped" plates**.
4. **Vehicle, insurance and driver standards.** These come from the LDH MCO Manual, Medical Transportation section, 2026-03-03 (https://www.ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/MCPP/3_9_26/2026-LDH-3_MCO_Manual_Medical_Transportation_2026-03-03.pdf):
   - Each vehicle must have a "for hire", "public" or "public handicapped" plate under La. R.S. 45:181 and 49:121, and must be licensed in the business name.
   - Auto liability of at least 25/50/25 split, or a $300,000 combined single limit. The certificate of insurance must say the coverage is for an NEMT vehicle and have a 30-day cancellation clause, and LDH must be named as additional insured.
   - Workers' compensation insurance as Louisiana law requires.
   - Annual criminal history checks on drivers (Louisiana State Police or FBI).
   - At least a five-panel drug screen, done annually.
   - Each vehicle is inspected and given a decal by the MCO or broker before use, and wheelchair vans must meet ADA requirements.
5. **Verida's intake requirements** (https://verida.com/louisiana-providers/):
   - A Request for Qualifications form plus driver and vehicle lists.
   - Verida takes applicants *"if there is a need for new transportation providers in the region"*.
   - Documents: current business license, liability insurance, driver credentials, criminal background checks, drug screens for owners and drivers, and motor vehicle records.
6. **Local occupational license.** An East Baton Rouge Parish occupational license is implied by Verida's "current business license" requirement. Confirming this was out of scope, so it is [UNCONFIRMED]. Special local NEMT permits exist in Orleans and Jefferson Parishes and in Shreveport, according to the legacy LDH HSS application (https://ldh.la.gov/assets/medicaid/hss/docs/nemt/hss_mt_licenseapplication.doc, 2015).

## 7. The e-commerce request

Nothing public explains it. Facts and hypotheses:

- **Fact:** the NPI lists a single taxonomy, NEMT (VAN). There is no Durable Medical Equipment taxonomy, which is what a mobility-equipment seller billing insurance would normally have. [VERIFIED]
- **Fact:** the new curamobility.org placeholder has an email-list signup ("updates, promotions"). It has no shop. [VERIFIED]
- **Hypothesis A:** "E-commerce" may mean **online booking with payment** for private-pay rides (prepaid trips, deposits, packages). This is the most likely reading for NEMT. [UNCONFIRMED]
- **Hypothesis B:** He may want to **sell mobility aids** (wheelchairs, walkers, lift accessories), given "Mobility" in the name. Retail cash sales need sales-tax registration. Billing Medicare or Medicaid for equipment would need DME enrollment and accreditation, which is a separate business line. [UNCONFIRMED]
- **Hypothesis C:** He may want **faith-based merchandise**, given the Bible-and-cross logo. [UNCONFIRMED]
- **Precedent:** competitor WE LIFT runs a shop and cart on its site.
- **Ask:** "What exactly would customers buy online: ride bookings, equipment, or merchandise?"

## Red flags / discrepancies

1. **Very new, pre-revenue footprint.** The LLC is about 1 month old (2026-08-20), the NPI about 4 weeks old (2026-08-26), and the domain was registered today. There are no reviews, GBP, social accounts or broker credentials yet. The intake says business comes from "Google search" and "Google Ads", but no public web presence exists yet.
2. **Two addresses.** The SOS and NPI mailing address is a Baton Rouge apartment (5121 Everett Ln, Apt/Bldg C). The NPI practice location is a St. Francisville road address. Neither should be published without the client's OK. Consider a service-area GBP with the address hidden.
3. **Statewide claim versus reality.** "Serves all of Louisiana" depends on broker assignments in each region. Brokers can turn down new providers where supply is adequate. Until broker contracts exist, use "based in the Baton Rouge area, serving [confirmed parishes]".
4. **Licensing is unproven.** There is no evidence yet of Medicaid enrollment, a MediTrans or Verida contract, an LPSC GLC, for-hire plates or NEMT insurance. Do not claim "licensed" or "Medicaid provider".
5. **Domain ambiguity.** curamobility.com (2024) versus curamobility.org (2026-09-22). Confirm which one he controls.
