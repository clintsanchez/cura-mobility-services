# Elementor template kit shortlist (Envato Elements)

*2026-09-23. I ran 17 searches in the Envato Elements "Template Kits" category (medical transportation, senior care, home care, caregiver, wheelchair, taxi, shuttle, patient transport and more), which surfaced about 130 kits. **No NEMT-specific kit exists.** The best fits are ride-booking kits and senior/home-care kits. Judged on page structure and ease of handoff, not colors. Screenshots are in `.playwright-mcp/kits/` (gitignored).*

The Medical Multipurpose Webkit and Transport Webkit are available for extra sections. BD Library Awesome's site search returned nothing for these terms, so its catalog would need browsing by category.

## Notes
- **Hybrid option:** all kits are on the same subscription. Use FleetGo for the booking, fleet and pricing structure, and borrow warm care sections (families, testimonials, how it works) from Grandcare or PelitaCare.
- Turnkey preference: Pro-native kits (FleetGo, PelitaCare) avoid add-on plugins, since Michael will own Elementor Pro.
- Every kit uses Envato demo images. Replace them or license them, localize any kept images, and purge the demo content before launch.
- Register the chosen kit(s) to the Cura project in Envato Elements before download.
