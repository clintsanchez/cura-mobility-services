## 1. The headline

1. **The organic field is empty of local providers.** In every Baton Rouge NEMT query tested, page 1 is Uber Health templates, government pages (brla.gov, LDH), brokers (MediTrans), directories (Yelp, findhelp, BBB, care.com) and national template sites (Freedmans Health, MedicalRide, RideSenior). **No Baton Rouge-area provider's own site ranks.** A real site with specific service pages can compete for organic results within months.
2. **The map pack is more competitive than we first thought.** A live Maps check on 2026-09-22 found two strong players the earlier research missed:
   - **Mitresonz Transportation Services LLC: 5.0 stars, 185 reviews.** This is the real review leader.
   - **Tender Kare Transportation (Denham Springs): 3.3 stars, 74 reviews.**
   - Hammond (4.5 stars, 33 reviews) still ranks #1 for the core query. LMT Medical Transportation (5.0 stars, 14 reviews) and 1st Call Transport (4.1 stars, 28 reviews) are also present.
3. **Competitors use weak categories.** Almost every map-pack listing's primary category is the generic **"Transportation service"**. A few use **"Handicapped transportation service"** (Exquisite, Stick With Us). None showed a medical-specific category. A correctly categorized Cura profile has a real relevance edge.
4. **Wheelchair and dialysis are the most open topics.** No local provider has a dialysis page. The "wheelchair van" results are mostly van dealers.
5. **Skip statewide terms.** "Non emergency medical transportation louisiana" belongs to LDH, the brokers and the health plans. This also fits the rule against claiming "statewide" coverage.

## 4. Google Business Profile recommendations

- **Type:** service-area business with the **address hidden**. Don't expose the Baton Rouge apartment or the St. Francisville address. ⏸ #6
- **Primary category:** the most specific medical option the live category picker offers ("Medical transportation service" or a non-emergency variant, whichever exists). Check it in the live picker at setup. Competitors mostly use generic "Transportation service", which leaves us an edge. If a wheelchair-accessible vehicle is confirmed, add **"Handicapped transportation service"** as a secondary. Don't add "Ambulance service" or any tier Cura isn't equipped and licensed for.
- **Name:** "Cura Mobility Services". No keywords in the name.
- **Service area:** Start with East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston and West Baton Rouge. Add Iberville, Pointe Coupee and West Feliciana only if Michael confirms them. ⏸ #7
- **Services:** list the same services as the website's service pages.
- **Reviews:** set up a text or QR review request after every ride from day one. Target 3–5 reviews in month 1, spread across the month, then 2–4 a month on an ongoing basis. Respond to every review.
- **Suspension risks:** a residential address, a thin new profile in a healthcare-adjacent category, and inconsistent name/address/phone details (two addresses are on public record). Lock one NAP before creating any listing.

## 5. Listings and links, in order
1. **Core listings:** Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor.
2. **Health and caregiver directories:**
   - findhelp.org, which discharge planners use.
   - MedicalRide.org. It lists no local providers, so it's an open slot.
   - care.com and Caring.com.
   - Louisiana 211 and Capital Area Agency on Aging. The research couldn't confirm the right domain or portal for either; verify before submitting.
3. **Outreach once insurance and vehicle documents are in hand:** DaVita and Fresenius dialysis centers, and the discharge planners at BR General, OLOL, Ochsner BR and Woman's (verify names first). Also local chambers; Tender Kare uses its Livingston Chamber badge.
4. **Medicaid:** broker network directories come later, and only if he enrolls.

## 6. Site-level must-haves
- Schema: LocalBusiness/MedicalBusiness with areaServed, plus Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList. No competitor showed any.
- Click-to-call plus a short Request a Ride form (pickup, drop-off, date and time, mobility needs, return trip). Apply the blog CTA standard in CLAUDE.md.
- Show hours and required booking notice prominently. Several competitors are weekday-only, so early-morning or Saturday dialysis coverage is worth leading with if it's real.
- Differentiate on reliability (on-time pickup, a call or text when the driver is on the way). Competitor reviews cite no-shows and long waits. **Don't name competitors or invent statistics.**

## 7. Accuracy flags (read before writing copy)

  - Our earlier research (LDH page) has Verida at **225-726-2800**.

- **Facility names** (hospitals, dialysis centers): verify each one before it's published.
- **Standing rules:** no "licensed", no "Medicaid provider", no "statewide", no tenure claims, no public address.
