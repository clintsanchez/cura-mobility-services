## 5. Openings for Cura [INFERRED; confirm with client]
- Local 225 number and real presence vs out-of-area templates.
- Wheelchair-accessible vans, if confirmed (RideSenior can't; Uber outsources).
- Full-structure site from day one vs one-page competitors.
- Reliability as the promise: on-time pickup, call/text when on the way. Don't name competitors or invent stats.
- Hours: several competitors are weekdays only. Early/Saturday dialysis coverage, if real, is worth leading with.
- Local specificity (real hospitals, dialysis centers, parishes) vs templates.
- Nobody pairs clear pricing with online booking for wheelchair rides.

## 6. Avoid
- Targeting "non emergency medical transportation louisiana" (government/brokers own it).
- Statewide or national-scale claims.
- Any Medicaid/MediTrans network claim before proof. No tenure claims. Verify every facility name before publishing.
